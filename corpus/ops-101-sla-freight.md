## 1. Scope

This agreement covers scheduled and on-demand temperature-controlled freight
operated by Halcyon Logistics for contracted customers in the EU and UK. It does
not cover customs brokerage, warehousing beyond 72 hours, or last-mile delivery
subcontracted to a third party.

## 2. Definitions

- **Delivery window**: the agreed arrival range, expressed in local time.
- **On-time delivery**: arrival inside the delivery window, with a signed proof
  of delivery (POD).
- **Temperature excursion**: any logger reading outside the product range for
  longer than the tolerance stated in OPS-110.
- **Business hours**: 08:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

## 3. Service levels

| Metric | Target | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-time delivery | 97.5% | monthly, per customer |
| Temperature integrity | 99.4% of shipments | monthly, per lane |
| Proof of delivery capture | 98.0% within 30 minutes of arrival | daily |
| Exception notification | within 4 business hours of confirmation | per event |

Notify the affected customer of a confirmed exception within 4 business hours.

Service levels are measured on completed shipments. Shipments cancelled by the
customer are excluded from the denominator.

## 4. Credits

3.1 If on-time delivery falls below the target in a calendar month, the customer
receives a service credit of 2% of the monthly freight spend for every full
percentage point below target, capped at 12% of that month's spend.

3.2 Credits are issued automatically on the next invoice. They are not paid in
cash and cannot be offset against a disputed invoice.

3.3 A credit request must reference the affected shipment identifiers. Requests
without shipment identifiers are rejected.

## 5. Claims

5.1 Cargo claims must be submitted within 14 calendar days of delivery, or of
the scheduled delivery date when the shipment never arrived.

5.2 Claims submitted after the window are closed without review. The only
exception is a documented legal hold notified by the customer's legal team.

5.3 Halcyon acknowledges a claim within 2 business days and issues a disposition
within 15 business days.

## 6. Exclusions

Halcyon is not liable for delays caused by force majeure, customer-caused loading
delays, incorrect address data, or product that was already outside its range at
pickup and documented as such on the collection note.

## 7. Review cadence

Commercial Operations reviews this agreement every six months with each customer
above EUR 250,000 of annual freight spend. Changes take effect on the first day
of the following quarter.
