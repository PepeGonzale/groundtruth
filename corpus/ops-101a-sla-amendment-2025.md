## 1. Purpose

This amendment updates the service levels and claim windows in OPS-101. It does
not replace OPS-101 in full. Where the two documents disagree, this amendment
prevails from its effective date.

## 2. Updated service levels

From 1 April 2025 the on-time delivery target is **98.2%**, measured monthly per
customer. The temperature integrity target remains 99.4% of shipments. Proof of
delivery capture remains 98.0% within 30 minutes of arrival.

The exception notification target is unchanged: a confirmed exception must be
notified within 4 business hours.

## 3. Credit schedule

2.1 The credit rate increases to 3% of monthly freight spend for every full
percentage point below the 98.2% target, capped at 15% of that month's spend.

2.2 Credits below EUR 250 are not issued. They are accumulated and applied when
the balance exceeds EUR 250.

## 4. Claims window

4.1 Cargo claims must be submitted within **21 calendar days** of delivery, or of
the scheduled delivery date when the shipment never arrived.

4.2 The acknowledgement target stays at 2 business days. The disposition target
stays at 15 business days.

## 5. Transition

Shipments delivered before 1 April 2025 are measured against OPS-101. The
amendment applies to shipments delivered on or after that date. No retroactive
credits are issued for the first quarter of 2025.
